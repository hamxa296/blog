# GIKI Freshman Guide — Master Content & Design Specification
> **Document Purpose:** Complete, hierarchical master content specification and design brief for the **GIKI Freshman Guide (GIKI Chronicles)**. Designed for handoff to student societies, media clubs, graphic designers, UI/UX teams, and publication committees for digital, web, mobile, and print design.

---

## 🎨 Creative & Design Guidelines

### Visual Identity & Theme
* **Primary Brand Tones:** Deep Nightfall (`#201e1c` / `#111111` / `#0A1931`), Electric Coral/Pink Accent (`#FD4378` / `#E11D48`), Soft Steel/Sky Blue (`#4A7FA7` / `#B3CFE5`), Crisp White (`#F6FAFD`), Muted Gray (`#9CA3AF`).
* **Typography Hierarchy:**
  * **Headings (Display):** Bold, modern sans-serif (e.g., *Outfit*, *Inter*, or *Clash Display*) or refined serif (e.g., *Lora*) for editorial warmth.
  * **Body & Descriptions:** Clean, legible sans-serif (*Inter*, *Roboto*) with generous line-height (`1.6` - `1.8`).
  * **Data & Monospace:** *JetBrains Mono* or *Fira Code* for IDs, timetables, and pricing.
* **Layout Paradigm:** Bento-Grid cards, expandable detail drawers/modals, interactive pill tabs, checklist cards, and high-contrast callout boxes for warnings and tips.
* **Hero Image Mapping:** 12 official banner illustrations/renders correspond to each section (`guide1.png` through `guide12.png`).

## Section 2: Student ID & Official Documents
* **Section ID:** `student-id-documents`
* **Section Header Asset:** `guide2new.png`
* **Icon:** FileText / Identification Card
* **Tag / Title:** Student ID & Documents
* **Short Description:** Essential documents, attestation checklists, and ID card issuance for new students.

### 2.1 Overview & Importance
Getting your student ID card and completing your document verification is one of the very first mandatory tasks upon arriving at GIKI. Your student ID serves as your all-in-one digital passport across campus.

### 2.2 Mandatory Documents Checklist for Registration
Ensure you bring original documents along with at least **3 to 4 attested photocopies** of each:
- [ ] **Original CNIC** (Computerized National Identity Card) or **B-Form** (if under 18)
- [ ] **Original Matriculation Certificate** (SSC / O-Level Equivalence)
- [ ] **Original Intermediate Certificate** (HSSC / A-Level Equivalence)
- [ ] **Original Detailed Marks Certificate (DMC)** for both Matric and Intermediate
- [ ] **Character Certificate** issued by your previous college/school
- [ ] **Medical Fitness Certificate** (signed by a registered medical practitioner)
- [ ] **8 Recent Passport-size Photographs** (strictly with light blue background)
- [ ] **Copy of Official Admission Offer Letter**
- [ ] **Paid Fee Challan Bank Receipt** (Student & University copy)

### 2.3 Student ID Card: Capabilities & Usage
Your GIKI Smart Card / Student ID is required daily for:
1. **Library Services:** Entry verification and automated book checkout/returns.
2. **Mess Operations:** Digital punch-in for breakfast, lunch, and dinner meal tracking.
3. **Campus Gate Security:** In-and-out identification at main security turnstiles.
4. **Academic Attendance:** Biometric / barcode attendance verification in select lecture halls and labs.
5. **Sports Complex & Facility Access:** Gym, squash courts, and equipment rental access.

### 2.4 ID Card Issuance Process
* **Timeline:** ID cards are processed and distributed during the first week of orientation.
* **Collection Venue:** Administration Block / Student Affairs Office (schedule announced via email).
* **Cost:** Free of charge for all newly admitted freshmen.
* **Verification:** Must present original CNIC / B-Form at the collection desk.

### 2.5 Document Safety Protocol
* Store all original credentials in a durable, waterproof document binder.
* Keep high-resolution PDF scans backed up to Google Drive and your phone.
* Never hand over original documents to unauthorized individuals.
* Keep personal documents locked inside your dorm room cupboard.

### 2.6 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
* **Q: What if I don't have my CNIC yet?**
  * **A:** You can use your computerized B-Form issued by NADRA. However, apply for your Smart CNIC promptly as official degree verifications require it.
* **Q: Can I get my ID card before orientation?**
  * **A:** No. Physical identity cards are issued only after in-person biometric and document verification during orientation week.
* **Q: What should I do if I lose my student ID?**
  * **A:** Immediately inform the administration office and security. Fill out a replacement requisition form and pay the designated replacement fee (processing takes 3–5 working days).
* **Q: Do I need to bring original certificates or just photocopies?**
  * **A:** You must bring both. The administration inspects the originals in person and retains the attested photocopies in your permanent student file.

### 2.7 Helpful Reminders & Warnings
> ⚠️ **Warning:** Never lend your student ID to anyone. It is strictly non-transferable and linked to your biometric records.
> ⚠️ **Warning:** Ensure all educational documents are properly attested by IBCC (for O/A levels or foreign boards) prior to arrival.

## Section 5: Finances, Fees & Daily Budgeting
* **Section ID:** `finances`
* **Section Header Asset:** `guide5.png`
* **Icon:** CreditCard / Wallet
* **Tag / Title:** Finances
* **Short Description:** Managing your money, tuition fee challans, mess billing, and monthly expenses.

### 5.1 Fee Structure & Official GIKI Portal
All official tuition fees, hostel room charges, and security deposits are billed through the GIKI Student Portal.
* **Notification:** You will receive an official email before each semester detailing the fee challan.
* **Payment Mechanism:** Generate the 3-part bank challan from your portal and deposit at any Habib Bank Limited (HBL) branch nationwide or via designated mobile banking apps.
* **Deadlines:** Surcharges apply for late payments. Always download your fee clearance slip for records.

### 5.2 Monthly Student Budget Breakdown
A realistic monthly student budget at GIKI (outside of fixed semester tuition and hostel room fees) typically ranges between **PKR 7,000 to PKR 18,000**:
* **Mess & Food:** PKR 4,000 – 9,000 (depending on mess consumption and opt-outs)
* **Tuck Shop, Cafes & Late Night Chai:** PKR 2,000 – 4,000
* **Weekend Shuttle Transport:** PKR 1,500 – 3,500 (if traveling home 2–3 times a month)
* **Academic Printing, Stationery & Laundry:** PKR 1,000 – 1,500

### 5.3 Smart Financial Survival Tips
1. **Track Every Rupee:** Use a mobile expense tracker (e.g., Spendee, Wallet) to avoid running out of cash by the 20th of the month.
2. **Leverage Mess Opt-Out:** If you plan to eat at TUC or travel home over the weekend, log your mess opt-out on the portal before the 2:00 PM deadline to avoid being billed for uneaten food.
3. **Carpool & Shuttle Sharing:** Share local cabs to Rawalpindi or Topi market with hostel mates to split fares.
4. **ATM Availability:** The HBL ATM on campus can occasionally run out of cash on festive weekends. Keep emergency cash (PKR 3,000–5,000) stored safely in your room.

### 5.4 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
* **Q: How do I pay my semester fees?**
  * **A:** Download the challan from the GIKI portal, pay at any HBL branch or online via supported banking channels, and submit the stamped student verification copy to the accounts office.
* **Q: What are the typical monthly living expenses?**
  * **A:** Aside from university tuition, students typically spend between Rs. 7,000 to Rs. 15,000 per month on mess food, cafe snacks, and weekend bus travel.

### 5.5 Warnings
> ⚠️ **Financial Warning:** Avoid excessive spending in your initial month. Freshmen often overspend on impulse cafe visits and deliveries during orientation week.

### 6.1 Room Allotment & Infrastructure
* **Allocation:** Rooms are allotted by the hostel administration upon arrival during Orientation Week.
* **Room Capacity:** Freshman rooms are typically double-occupancy (shared by two students).
* **Furnishings Provided by University:**
  * 2 Single wooden beds with standard mattresses
  * 2 Individual study desks and study chairs
  * 2 Built-in wooden storage cupboards/wardrobes with hanging rails
  * Ceiling fans and window curtains
  * High-speed LAN internet port and wall power sockets

### 6.2 Dorm Room Video & Media Tours
Official video tours are accessible to help you visualize room dimensions and storage options:
* **Boys Hostel Room Tour:** Video walkthrough of a standard double room in Hostel 11/12 (`boys-room-tour.mp4`).
* **Girls Hostel Room Tour:** Video walkthrough of the New Girls Hostel double-occupancy room (`girls-room-tour.mp4`).

### 6.3 Roommate Etiquette & Dorm Culture
Hostel life is one of the most transformative parts of the GIKI experience. To maintain harmony:
1. **Establish Ground Rules Early:** Discuss sleep schedules, lights-out times, music volume, and room visitor policies.
2. **Maintain Cleanliness:** Sweep your room regularly; do not leave food leftovers overnight to prevent ants and insects.
3. **Respect Belongings:** Always ask permission before borrowing chargers, stationery, or clothing.
4. **Quiet Hours:** Keep hallway noise to a minimum past 11:00 PM during exam seasons.

### 6.4 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
* **Q: Can I change my room or roommate?**
  * **A:** Official room swap requests are generally restricted during your first semester. Changes are reviewed by the Chief Warden only under exceptional circumstances.
* **Q: What is the hostel mess system like?**
  * **A:** Each hostel block is linked to a dining hall providing three cooked meals daily with weekly rotating menus.

### 6.5 Helpful Reminders & Warnings
> ⚠️ **Important:** Keep your room door locked whenever both you and your roommate are away. Always lock your personal wardrobe with a solid padlock.

## Section 7: Weekend Travel & Transport Shuttle System
* **Section ID:** `weekend-travel`
* **Section Header Asset:** `guide7new.png`
* **Icon:** Bus / Navigation
* **Tag / Title:** Weekend Travel
* **Short Description:** How to travel home, book campus weekend shuttle buses, and coordinate carpools.

### 7.1 GIKI Official Weekend Shuttle Service
GIKI operates sponsored weekend luxury air-conditioned coaches every Friday afternoon to facilitate students visiting family in major nearby cities.

#### Shuttle Timetable & Routes
* **Departure Time:** Every Friday at **4:00 PM Sharp** (Buses arrive for boarding at 3:30 PM).
* **Departure Station on Campus:** Girls Hostel (GH) Gate & Hostel-9 Parking Bay.

| Destination City | Ticket Price | On-Campus Departure Spot | City Arrival / Drop-off Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rawalpindi / Islamabad** | **PKR 350** | GH / Hostel-9 | Road Master Adda / G-9/4 Peshawar Morr |
| **Peshawar** | **PKR 350** | GH / Hostel-9 | Bagh-e-Naran, Hayatabad |
| **Abbottabad** | **PKR 400** | GH / Hostel-9 | GO Petroleum Filling Station, Main City |

* **Return Journey:** Sunday evening coaches depart from city pickup points back to GIKI campus (schedule shared on ticket).

### 7.2 Ticket Booking Protocol
* **Booking Window:** Tickets go on sale **every Thursday morning** at the Transport Office (located directly behind the campus laundry shop).
* **Payment Mode:** **CASH ONLY**. Exact change is strongly encouraged.
* **Capacity Limit:** First-come, first-served. Queues start early on Thursday mornings!

### 7.3 Student Carpools & WhatsApp Ride-Sharing
If you miss the shuttle or need to travel at different times:
* Each hostel batch runs a WhatsApp "Ride Share" or "Carpool" group where seniors and students driving personal cars offer spare seats at fuel-split rates.
* Private cabs can be hired collectively from Topi Bazaar.

### 7.4 Security & Curfew Rules for Travel
* **All Students:** Must sign the hostel departure ledger before leaving campus.
* **Female Students:** Must obtain an authorized digital/physical **Gate Pass** countersigned by the Girls Hostel Warden prior to passing main campus security gates.

### 7.5 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
* **Q: Can I book shuttle tickets online or via mobile app?**
  * **A:** No, tickets must be purchased in person at the Transport Office behind the laundry shop.
* **Q: What happens if I miss the official Friday shuttle?**
  * **A:** Coordinate a ride-share via student WhatsApp groups or travel via public transport from Topi Adda to Swabi/Peshawar Morr.

### 7.6 Warnings
> ⚠️ **Travel Warning:** Always travel in groups when taking off-campus commercial transit, especially during late night hours. Avoid unauthorized private drivers outside the campus gate.

## Section 8: Mess & Campus Dining
* **Section ID:** `mess-and-food`
* **Section Header Asset:** `guide8.png`
* **Icon:** UtensilsCrossed / Coffee
* **Tag / Title:** Mess & Food
* **Short Description:** Daily hostel dining hall timings, menu system, mess-off procedures, and campus cafes.

### 8.1 Dining Hall Schedules
The central hostel mess serves three hot meals daily on a rotating weekly menu:
* 🍳 **Breakfast:** `07:00 AM – 09:00 AM` (Eggs, Parathas, Chana, Toast, Tea)
* 🍛 **Lunch:** `12:30 PM – 02:30 PM` (Rice, Daal, Sabzi, Biryani, Chicken Curry, Salad)
* 🍗 **Dinner:** `07:30 PM – 09:30 PM` (Qorma, Karahi, Pulao, Seasonal Dishes, Sweet Desserts)

### 8.2 Mess In / Mess Out Rules (Billing Control)
* To avoid being charged for meals when eating out or traveling home, you must submit a **"Mess Out"** request on the student portal.
* **Cutoff Time:** Submissions must be logged before **2:00 PM** for dinner and the following day’s breakfast/lunch.

### 8.3 Campus Eateries & Cafes (Beyond the Mess)
When you crave a change from mess food:
1. **Hot & Spicy (Located at TUC):**
   * *Specialty:* The most popular fast-food joint on campus. Famous for chicken paratha rolls, zinger burgers, club sandwiches, and fries. Open till late at night.
2. **Ayan & Raju (Located at TUC):**
   * *Specialty:* Authentic Desi cuisine, including chicken karahi, handi, daal mash, naan, and BBQ tikka. Great for group dinners with friends.
3. **GIKI Cafe (Under the Main Auditorium):**
   * *Specialty:* Managed by GIKI Administration. A relaxed student hangout famous for chai, samosas, chicken rolls, and nuggets. Offers budget-friendly dinners at rates cheaper than the hostel mess.
4. **Juice Corners & Fruit Shops (TUC):**
   * Fresh seasonal juices, fruit chaat, milkshakes, and snacks.

### 8.4 Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
* **Q: Can I bring outside food into the mess hall?**
  * **A:** No, dining hall regulations prohibit bringing food from outside cafes into the mess area.
* **Q: Are there vegetarian options available?**
  * **A:** Yes, the rotating mess schedule always features daal, vegetables, and beans alongside meat options.
* **Q: Can I completely opt out of the mess?**
  * **A:** Yes, daily and extended opt-outs are supported through the online portal with appropriate advance notice.

### 8.5 Warnings
> ⚠️ **Reminder:** Always remember to mess out before leaving campus for the weekend; otherwise, you will be billed for all meals during your absence.

## Section 10: Academic Survival & Campus Life
* **Section ID:** `survival-tips`
* **Section Header Asset:** `guide10new.png`
* **Icon:** Sparkles / Lightbulb
* **Tag / Title:** Survival Tips
* **Short Description:** In-depth advice on cracking academics, GPA management, beating homesickness, and digital tools.

### 10.1 Academic Rigor & Time Management
* **Never Skip Lectures & Labs:** GIKI moves fast. Falling behind by two weeks makes midterms overwhelming.
* **Lab Submissions:** Lab reports carry heavy continuous assessment weight. Submit on time.
* **Form Peer Study Groups:** Join study circles with classmates from your dorm to exchange lecture notes and solve problem sets.
* **Office Hours:** Faculty members have weekly consultation hours. Don't hesitate to ask questions.

### 10.2 Overcoming Homesickness
Living away from family for the first time is daunting for many students:
* Stay physically active: Use the gymnasium, sports grounds, or take evening campus walks with friends.
* Keep in daily contact with parents via video calls.
* Reach out to your **Resident Advisor (RA)** or friendly seniors in your hostel — they’ve experienced the exact same emotions.

### 10.3 Essential Digital Apps for Every GIKI Student
1. **GIKI Student Portal:** For grades, timetable, fee challans, and attendance percentages.
2. **Microsoft Teams:** Primary platform for faculty lecture slides, online tutorials, and assignment submissions.
3. **WhatsApp:** Vital for hostel section groups, class announcements, and rideshares.
4. **Google Drive & Docs:** Essential for collaborative group project reports and cloud backup of assignments.
5. **Casio fx-ES Emulator / Desmos:** For engineering calculation checks.

### 10.4 Helpful Reminders & Warnings
> ⚠️ **Key Advice:** Don't isolate yourself in your dorm room. Go outside, explore the campus, make friends, and balance hard work with healthy recreational breaks.
